## `> FEATURED_PROJECTS.json`

- 🕹️ **Document Forgery Detection** — 90% accuracy across 17,386 samples
  Weighted ensemble — EfficientNet-B0, a Convolutional Autoencoder, and a Siamese Network — reaching **88.9% F1, 95% AUC**. Recovered from two failed training runs via data-quality fixes, Gaussian filtering, histogram equalisation, and early stopping.
  `Python` `PyTorch` `EfficientNet-B0` `OpenCV` `FastAPI` `Streamlit`
  [Repo](https://github.com/shravanrt19/Document-forgery-detector)

- 🕹️ **Competitive Intelligence Platform** — 92.4% competitor discovery accuracy
  End-to-end LLM pipeline — Llama 3 + Groq API — that identifies competitors, scrapes web intelligence, scores relevance, and generates strategic insights. Async FastAPI + PostgreSQL backend, full reports in under 5 seconds, deployed via Docker on Railway with a Next.js frontend on Vercel.
  `Llama 3` `Groq API` `FastAPI` `Next.js` `PostgreSQL` `Docker`
  [Repo](https://github.com/shravanrt19/AI-Competitive-Intelligence)

- 🕹️ **AI Safety Zone Mapper — Guardian Mode** — 🏆 Top 10 of 76 teams, final round of 6 — HACKOCOLYPSE 2.0
  Led a 4-person team to build a women's safety app: GPS risk scoring across 500+ geo-zones, Guardian Mode privacy alerts, and SOS escalation in under 3 seconds.
